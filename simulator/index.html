Ronin MUD — Group Damage Simulator

Simulates a group of 5 characters fighting a training dummy for 600 seconds, using every skill and spell available to their subclass (subclass level 5, character level 50).
Select your group and press Simulate.
All subclasses (solo, same inputs — group buffs only affect themselves)
Model assumptions (from ronin/src): combat ticks every 3s (PULSE_VIOLENCE) = 200 rounds in 600s; spell/buff durations are measured in 60s ticks (affect_update runs on PULSE_TICK, comm.c:1009), so 1 tick = 20 rounds. Every character makes their basic multi-strike attack every round, plus at most one extra skill/spell/buff action per round. All skills share one lag timer (WAIT_STATE blocks every command, comm.c:797): after any skill, no other skill can be used until its lag elapses — circle 3 rounds, banzai 1 round, all others 2 rounds. Backstab only works on an unengaged target, so it can be used once per fight. Quick Recovery (Rogue SC5) cuts skill lag by 1 with chance(number(50,75)) ≈ expected −0.625 rounds, letting the Rogue circle ~84 times in 200 rounds. So headbutt and flank must alternate (~50 each = ~100 total uses); only banzai (1-round lag) can fire every round, and it also grants one extra full basic-attack round on use. Rogue’s Circle can Trip the target (SC4, toggle assumed on): a separate number(1,111) check after a successful circle that stuns on success. Target is a standing, full-HP dummy: every attack has a 5% chance to miss (95% hit rate; a miss stops the multi-strike chain), no saving throws (all subclass spells use SAVING_NONE). Stunning skills (Pummel, Backfist, Tigerkick, Headbutt, Smite) leave the target STUNNED so the next attack deals ×2 damage (Iron Fist Punch ×1.55 expected: 67% sitting ×1.333 + 33% stun ×2); Bash sets it resting (×1.5); Batter sets it sitting (×1.333); Clobber averages ×2.21 over its random victim states. Aid (Chanter SC4) is cast on the whole group: +10% of all damage to all 5 members for 5 ticks (100 rounds). War Chant ×1.2 (dummy isn't war-chantered), Magic Armament +2d5, Might +3 damroll 20 ticks, Righteousness +1d4 10 ticks, Rage ×1.5 all damage 5 ticks (cast as soon as affordable), Frenzy ×1.5 weapon damage, Tranquility +3 hit/dam 6 ticks, Mystic Swiftness 4 ticks, Blade Dance deals 350 + one full basic attack and refreshes Bladesinging (+40 damroll) for 10 rounds, each Shadow Wraith shadow lasts 10 ticks (200 rounds). Buffs don't stack: Aid, War Chant and Divine Hammer can only be recast after they expire. Bandit's backstab can only be used once, at the start of the fight. Characters start with one tick of mana regen (the Mana_Regen input; cap 1000) and gain Mana_Regen every 60s. Skill success uses the source formulas (e.g. number(1,137) − DEX_APP ≤ learned) with the Skills/Dexterity/Wisdom inputs; expected values are used instead of random rolls so results are stable. Crits: Nomads 20% + 2%/Dex over 18 (+10 Awareness, +10 Berserk), Ronin Sento Kata 20%; crits double weapon damage only. Multi-strikes use the DUAL/TRIPLE/QUAD skill checks (Skills input) with Juggernaut (Warlord SC4) and Hostile (Gladiator SC5) toggles; Ninja always gets a 2nd hit. Clerics and Bards are assumed under Haste: chance(30 + DEX_APP) of an extra basic attack each round (a missed haste attack stops the chain). Passive toggles assumed on: Fury (both Paladins ×2.5 weapon damage), Berserk, Frenzy (×1.5), Awareness, Hostile, Vehemence, Trusty Steed, Dragon form (Druid). A pummel'd/stunned victim takes ×2 on the next hit (×5 total with Fury). Smite's steed trample is an extra basic attack at ×2 on the stunned victim and leaves it resting. Snipe never fires vs a full-HP target. Excluded (no corpse / defensive / no dummy interaction): Trophy, Wall of Thorns, Desecrate, Blade Barrier, Blackmantle, Orb, Fortification, Evasion, Defend, Riposte, Feint, Camaraderie, heals.
